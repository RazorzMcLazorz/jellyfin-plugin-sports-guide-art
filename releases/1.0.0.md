## 1.0.0 (2026-11-24)

First release. Requires Jellyfin 12.4 or later.

### Features

- Fills Live TV programs that have no art. It tries the same title on the same channel first, so `Upcoming:`, `Pregame:` and `Postgame:` slots get the game's poster, thumbnail and backdrop. Then it tries the same title on any channel, then art remembered from earlier guide refreshes.
- Generates a matchup poster (2:3) and landscape image (16:9) for "Team A vs Team B" titles that have no art anywhere, with team logos and colors.
- Team logos come from ESPN (on by default, with a league list you can edit) or TheSportsDB (optional, takes your API key). You choose which is tried first.
- Every copied image, generated image and logo is cached under the server's data folder, so art survives guide refreshes and provider URL changes. Unused files are deleted after 30 days, and you can change that.
- Runs automatically after every Refresh Guide, at startup, and every 6 hours. A **Fill sports guide art** task appears under Scheduled Tasks.
- Settings page: title prefixes to ignore, matching options, channel filters, logo sources, cache retention, and **Save and run now**.
- Never replaces art your provider supplies. A generated image is swapped for real art once real art appears.
